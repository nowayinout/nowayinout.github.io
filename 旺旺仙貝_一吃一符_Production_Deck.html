Section 04
Music Direction
音樂指引
Composer Direction

The score should feel like it exists between two worlds: the ancient ritual pulse of Taiwan's folk tradition (erhu, guqin, taiko) and the vast cinematic scale of a Hans Zimmer–style orchestral arc. No electronic beats. No Western pop. The music must breathe — it should feel like wind over a mountain and smoke over incense.

00:00 – 00:09
Phase 1: Void / 空
Complete silence broken only by the ambient hum of city height (wind at altitude). At 00:05, a single low guqin string plucked once. Reverb fills the space. Anticipation without resolution.
00:10 – 00:29
Phase 2: The Pulse / 脈動
Taiko drums begin — one beat every two seconds, like a pilgrim's footstep. Erhu melody enters, mournful and searching. Tempo: ♩= 52 BPM. The pilgrimage is ancient. The scale is vast. The music must honor both.
00:20 – 00:29
Phase 2b: Silence Drop / 靜
Mid-scene, at the kneeling shot (00:23), ALL MUSIC STOPS. Only ambient sound: chanting voices, distant firecrackers, the shuffle of feet. Hold for 6 seconds. This silence is the loudest moment in the film.
00:30 – 00:44
Phase 3: Internal / 內在
Low cello enters, single sustained note under Alex's reflection. No percussion. The music is now inside him. A solo piano motif — 5 notes, repeating — represents belief forming.
00:45 – 00:49
Phase 4: The Bite / 脆響
The CRACK of the cracker is designed as a musical event — it lands on the downbeat. A pizzicato string burst, bright and light. Then warmth: strings rise, xylophone sparkles. Brief, joyful, surprising.
00:50 – 01:01
Phase 5: The Collection / 集齊
Each of 6 blessings adds a new instrument layer. 財運: gold brass. 健康: flute. 愛情: strings soar. 長壽: choir hum. 人緣: marimba. 事業: full orchestra. By the 6th blessing, the score is at near-full power. BPM: 68 and climbing.
01:02 – 01:09
Phase 6: March / 前行
A quiet but resolute march rhythm. The climber is moving. The strings are warm, the brass steady. Not triumphant yet — purposeful.
01:10 – 01:21
Phase 7: The Ascent / 攀升
Full orchestral crescendo. Every instrument. Tempo: ♩= 88 BPM. Taiko returns — now powerful, not funereal. The music peaks at 01:20 (apex of bridge) → HARD CUT TO SILENCE. Wind only. Two piano notes.
01:22 – 01:30
Phase 8: Resolution / 圓滿
Under the closing quote: a single erhu phrase — the same 5-note motif from Phase 3, now played fully. Recognition. Completion. The loop closes. Fade to silence as brand card appears.
Section 05
Closing Quote Treatment
結尾金句設計
「有些力量來自肌肉，
有些力量來自相信。」
"Some strength comes from muscle.
Some strength comes from belief."
Typography Treatment

The quote should appear in a traditional Chinese calligraphic serif typeface (e.g., 文鼎細明, or custom brush-drawn). It fades in character by character — not word by word — from left to right. The characters 肌肉 appear in cool silver. The characters 相信 appear in warm 旺旺 gold (#C9A84C). The contrast is the whole point of the brand.

Delivery Note — Alex Honnold

Alex delivers this line slowly, with long pauses: 「有些力量——（停頓 1.5s）——來自肌肉，（停頓 2s）有些力量——（停頓 1s）——來自……相信。」 The word 相信 should nearly be exhaled. As if he is just realizing it himself, right now, on camera. That's the performance we're looking for — not an advertisement, but a confession.

Section 06
Production Notes
製作備注
📐 Aspect Ratio & Format
Primary: 2.39:1 Anamorphic (theatrical)
Deliverables: 16:9 for broadcast/digital; 9:16 vertical cut for social (Scenes 06–08 only); 1:1 square for Instagram.
🎨 Color Grade Reference
Shadow: deep blue-black (not crushed). Midtones: warm amber lean. Highlights: preserved, not blown. Reference films: Free Solo (NatGeo), Jiro Dreams of Sushi, Ted Lasso S3 Taiwan episode warm grade.
🎥 Camera Package
Primary: ARRI Alexa 35 with Leica Thalia anamorphic set. B-cam: Sony FX9 for handheld/documentary run. Drone: DJI Inspire 3 with Zenmuse X9-8K. All product shots: Canon 5D Macro rig.
📍 Location Requirements
大甲鎮瀾宮 (遶境 primary): April shoot. 淡江大橋 (Danjiang): blue hour to golden hour. Jiufen, Taroko, Sun Moon Lake, Tainan: day shoots. All locations require advance permits and crowd coordination.
🗣 Language / Voiceover
Alex delivers VO in English; subtitled in Traditional Chinese. Vendor dialogue in Taiwanese Hokkien (台語), subtitled in Mandarin. Alex's on-camera lines in Mandarin (coached delivery with subtitle confirmation).
⏱ Cut Pacing Reference
Scenes 01–07: average shot length 3–5s (documentary patience). Scene 08 montage: 1.5s per beat. Scenes 09–10: return to 4–6s. Final scene: single 4s take, no cuts. The resting pace must make the final quote feel earned.
Director's Intent Statement

This film is not about a man eating a rice cracker. It is about two different kinds of courage recognizing each other across a cultural divide. Alex Honnold climbs mountains by eliminating fear through mastery. Taiwan's believers march by transforming fear into faith. These are mirror images of the same human act: choosing to move forward when every instinct says stop. 旺旺仙貝's 一吃一符 is the edible symbol that sits at this intersection — a bite of rice, a whisper of prayer, a small daily act of belief. The film should feel inevitable. Like we were always going to end up here, on top of this bridge, with this thought.
